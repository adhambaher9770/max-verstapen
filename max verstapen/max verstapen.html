About Max Verstappen

Max Verstappen is a Dutch racing driver who competes in Formula One for Red Bull Racing.

Max Verstappen

Max Verstappen, the Red Bull driver and a four-time Formula One World Champion, won his first Formula One World Championship in 2021 and has since established himself as one of the most talented drivers in the sport. He was born on September 30, 1997, in Belgium and is the son of former Formula One driver Jos Verstappen. Max made his Formula One debut in 2015 with Scuderia Toro Rosso (now Racing Bulls) and quickly gained attention for his impressive performances on the track.

2023 Season

In the 2023 Formula One season, Max Verstappen continued to showcase his exceptional driving skills and determination. He secured 19 victories from 22 races and achieved a record-breaking streak of 10 consecutive wins, proving that he is one of the greatest Formula One drivers of all time.

Max Verstappen's Achievements

• Youngest Formula One Grand Prix winner(in the 2016 spanish grand prix).

• Four-time Formula One World Champion(2021, 2022, 2023, 2024).

• Record for most wins in a single season(19 wins).

• Record for most consecutive wins in one season (10 wins).

• First Dutch Formula One World Champion(2021).

• The Fastest lap in Formula One history (in the 2025 Italian Grand Prix).

Max Verstappen is one of the greatest drivers in Formula One history and a true legend of the sport.
